Planar NMOS bench

A compact model of one planar NMOS: set the gate stack, geometry, doping and contacts, and get R, C, delay, leakage and breakdown. Includes a 30 V-class HV device, a design optimizer and a verification suite against 1D/2D Poisson solvers.
