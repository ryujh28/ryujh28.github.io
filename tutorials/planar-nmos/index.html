Model equations and assumptions

Gate stack and threshold

EOT = tIL·3.9/kIL + tHK·3.9/kHK. Inversion capacitance uses Toxe = EOT + 0.4 nm (inversion-charge centroid) + poly-depletion width·3.9/11.7, evaluated at VDD.

Long-channel Vth = VFB + 2φF + qNAWdmax/Cox; n+ poly gives φms = −(Eg/2+φF), metal gives φm − (χ + Eg/2 + φF), χ = 4.05 eV. No fixed oxide charge, uniform channel doping (no halo profile).

Short-channel roll-off and DIBL, BSIM3 form: ΔVth = −DVT0·θ(DVT1)·(Vbi−2φF) − ETA0·θ(DSUB)·VDS, θ(D) = e−D·Leff/2λ + 2e−D·Leff/λ, λ = √(εSi/εox·EOT·Wdmax)·(Xj,ext/Wdmax)0.227. DVT0 = 4.08, DVT1 = 0.602, ETA0 = 0.695, DSUB = 1.10 and the Xj exponent were fitted to a 2D Poisson solver (see tests) and checked on held-out devices. Subthreshold swing factor with the Taur–Ning short-channel term: n = m·[1 + (11·EOT/Wdmax)·e−πLeff/2(Wdmax+3EOT)].

Drain current

BSIM3-style single-piece model: smoothed Vgsteff covering subthreshold to strong inversion, body factor m = 1 + 3Tox/Wdmax, velocity saturation with Esat = 2vsat/μeff, vsat(T) = 2.4×107/(1+0.8eT/600) cm/s. Channel-length modulation is not modeled beyond DIBL.

Universal mobility: μeff = 540·(T/300)−1.5 / [1 + (Eeff/1 MV/cm)1.85], Eeff = (Vgsteff + 2Vth + 0.2)/(6Toxe). No strain, no Coulomb term at low field.

Series R is solved self-consistently: I = f(VGS−IRS, VDS−2IRS) by bisection.

Capacitance

  • Channel: Cinv·W·Leff (off state: Cox in series with depletion).
  • Overlap: Cox·W·Lov per side.
  • Outer fringe (Shrivastava–Fitzpatrick quarter-circle arcs), split by medium, with the plug cutting the arcs only over the width it covers (Np·CD): (2W/π)ε0[kspln(Wsp/tox) + kILDln(rmax/Wsp)], rmax = min(Hg, plug gap).
  • Inner fringe (2εSiW/π)ln(1+Xj,ext/2tox), counted only in the off state (screened by inversion).
  • Plug–gate: parallel plate over facing height min(Hg, Hplug) × plug CD, spacer and ILD in series, plus Palmer edge fringes for the plug's width and height edges, added per edge, plus coupling from the plug above the gate top to the gate top surface (saturates one gap above the gate).
  • Junction: abrupt one-sided; bottom (deep S/D and extension to well) plus channel-side sidewall to NA. The other three sidewalls are assumed STI-bounded. Drain uses the large-signal average over 0→VDD reverse bias.

Resistance (per side)

  • Resistivity from Caughey–Thomas electron mobility, full ionization.
  • Channel → extension front end: over the overlap the gate-induced accumulation sheet (at VGS = VDD) and the extension sheet form a two-sheet transmission line, in parallel with Ng–Lynch edge spreading (2ρext/π)ln(0.75Xj,ext/2 nm). Coupling resistivity 0.2·ρextXj,ext calibrated to a 2D resistor solver. RS falls for the first few nm of overlap and then rises again.
  • Extension under spacer: ρextWsp/(W·Xj,ext). Deep S/D sheet from spacer edge to plug.
  • Contact: ρc ∝ exp(φB/E0), E0 = E00coth(E00/kT), m* = 0.3m0; prefactor calibrated to ρc = 1×10−7 Ω·cm² at 1×1020 cm−3, φB = 0.6 eV, 300 K. Plug footprint is CD², no transfer-length or current-crowding correction.
  • Gate: distributed Rg = Rsh·W/(3Lg), contacted at one end.

Speed metrics

  • Intrinsic delay CggVDD/Ion.
  • Discharge delay tpHL = CdrainVDD/(2Ieff), Ieff = [I(VDD,VDD/2) + I(VDD/2,VDD)]/2 (Na et al., IEDM 2002). Gate held at VDD, so no Miller multiplication; that enters with the inverter.
  • fT = gm/2πCgg; fmax = fT/2√[gds(Rg+RS) + 2πfTRgCgd].

Body bias, oxide charge, output conductance

Body effect through ψs = 2φF + VSB in Vth, the body factor and the SCE length. Fixed oxide charge shifts VFB by −qQf/Cox; interface traps add qDit to the subthreshold capacitance. Channel-length modulation ΔL = l·ln[1 + (VDS − VDSat)/(l·Esat)], l = √(3·EOT·Xj,ext), above threshold only; current × (1 + ΔL/Leff). The strength coefficient is an input (1 = textbook Ko form, not fitted; 0 turns CLM off). With CLM the default Ion is about 27% higher than without.

Impact ionisation and breakdown

  • Substrate current (Chan/Hu): Isub/ID = (Ai/Bi)(VDS−VDSat)e−Bil/(VDS−VDSat), l = 0.22·tox1/3Xj1/2; drain current includes the multiplication.
  • Junction avalanche: ionisation integral (Van Overstraeten–de Man coefficients with their temperature factor) over the field of a cylindrical junction edge of radius Xj.
  • Zener: junction band-to-band current reaches the criterion. Gated diode: GIDL reaches the criterion, or avalanche with the gate-induced field (VDG−Eg)/(3·EOT) added over √(3·EOT·Xj,ext).
  • Punch-through: VGS = 0 channel current (with DIBL) reaches the criterion.
  • On-state snapback: Isub·Rsub = 0.7 V. SRH generation q·ni·Wdep·A/(2τg) is added to Ioff. Pelgrom σVth = AVT/√(W·Leff), AVT ≈ 1 mV·µm per nm EOT × (NA/10¹⁸)¼.

HV device (30 V class)

  • Drain: shallow LDD drift from under the gate to an n⁺ placed a drift length Loff from the gate edge, and a deep n⁻ (DDD) under the n⁺ that overhangs toward the gate by Oddd.
  • Drift resistance: parallel sheets (LDD, and LDD + DDD over the overhang) thinned by the drift–well depletion at the local bias (JFET). Current per width J(E) = G·E/(1 + E/Es) + κ·S(E): ohmic with velocity saturation up to the Kirk current q·N·vsat·t, plus space-charge-limited conduction beyond it (κ = 2ε·vsat·t/ℓ). The overhang entry adds a two-sheet transmission-line term. Low-current drift R agrees with a 2D resistor within 4%.
  • Breakdown: gate-edge / drift-surface avalanche from a quasi-2D surface Poisson model (gate field, drift depletion, RESURF from the body), the LDD channel-side corner, and the n⁺/DDD bottom corner (layered cylindrical junction). Checked against a 2D Poisson + field-line ionisation reference: rms 7–17% by data set for LDD ≥ 3 × NA and oxide ≥ 35 nm.
  • Punch-through: no HV short-channel current model (Vth is long-channel). A 1D reach-through voltage across Leff (drift included) is reported as a conservative bound, below the 2D onset on all 48 reference devices, and is kept out of BVdss.
  • BVdss is defined with the body at the source (VSB = 0). Pass voltage: VDD − Vout = Vth(VSB + Vout). Field transistor: n⁺ poly over the field oxide with the channel-stop doping.

Layout footprint

Area per device pitch (Lg + 2·Lsd + SL)·(W + SW), with the isolation space shared half-and-half with neighbours. In contact-rule mode Lsd = plug gap + CD + enclosure, so shrinking the plug gap or CD also shrinks the active, the drain junction and the footprint.

Leakage

  • Gate direct tunnelling: Schuegraf–Hu for the SiO₂ layer (φB 3.1 eV, m* 0.40 m₀) with the WKB attenuation of a HfO₂-like high-k layer (1.5 eV, 0.18 m₀); oxide voltage split by EOT. On state: gate-to-channel over W·Leff. Off state: gate-to-drain through the overlap.
  • Junction band-to-band tunnelling (Sze & Ng) at the drain extension–channel sidewall and the S/D bottom, using the peak junction field at VDD.
  • GIDL: same tunnelling expression with the Chan et al. surface field (VDG − Eg)/(3·EOT) over the overlap plus 2 nm. Order-of-magnitude only.

Geometric clamps such as min(Hg, Hplug) give slope kinks (not jumps) in sweeps. Not modeled: quantum Vth shift, halo/retrograde profiles, narrow-width effects, self-heating. Treat outputs as trend and sensitivity estimates, not sign-off numbers.
